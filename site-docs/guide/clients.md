# Show your team what needs changing.

Your team handles the server and AI setup. Ask them for review access, then [connect the extension](/guide/chrome-extension).

## Point and describe

Click the pinned extension. Right-click the element, write what you expected, then **Save point**.

<Demo step="capture" />

## Check and send

**Review & send → Send feedback.** Check for private information before sharing.

<Demo step="send" />

When the team shares a fix, try the same page again and reply in the feedback thread.

::: details Received a guest link instead?
Open the link your team sent and use its review controls. You don’t need to install a server or configure an agent.
:::
