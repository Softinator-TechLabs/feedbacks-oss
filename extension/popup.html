How to pin, review and send
  1. Chrome toolbar → Extensions (puzzle piece) → pin Feedbacks.
  2. Open the website, then click the pinned Feedbacks icon to start review.
  3. Hover an element, right-click, write a note and choose Save point.
  4. Choose Review & send on the page to finalize and check the saved images.
  5. Choose Send & Return or Send & Open in the editor to share with your team.

Saved points stay on this browser until you send them.
