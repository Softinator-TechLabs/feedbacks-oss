A team server comes first. Your company installs Feedbacks once on its own infrastructure. Ask the owner for its address and your sign-in access. The extension connects to that server; it does not host one.

Server & account

In your team’s web app, open Help → Copy server URL, then paste it here. Use the Feedbacks server address, not the website you are reviewing. Changing servers requires approval on that server.

Full-page capture (optional)

P scrolls the page and captures additional images. Use it when the whole page matters. Visible capture already includes the saved original view for each point, including closed menus.

Keep page controls out of the way

Drag the grip to move the Feedbacks dock. Collapse the panel or hide it, then reopen the Chrome extension to restore it. Exit is always available on the dock. Pin the extension using Chrome’s puzzle menu for quick access.
