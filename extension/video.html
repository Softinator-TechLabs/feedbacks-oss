Recording & privacy

Recording stops at 5 minutes or 40 MiB. Audio is off unless selected. Enable Share tab audio in Chrome’s picker for website sound. Microphone access uses a separate permission prompt. Navigation in the selected tab continues recording; use this recorder to pause or stop if page controls are unavailable. Review or discard it before sending, especially if you selected another tab. Only your selected project can access an uploaded recording. Your server operator controls retention; Feedbacks has no automatic deletion timer.
