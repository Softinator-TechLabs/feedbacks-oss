Less describing.
More pointing.

Capture the visible page or the whole scroll. Circle the awkward spacing, highlight the copy, and number the steps. The screenshot, your comment and selected element context stay together.

  • Make the point visible. Pencil, arrows, shapes, text, highlighter and numbered steps. Add a sticker or your own image.
  • Keep long pages readable. Review full-page captures as individual sections or one continuous preview.
  • Take your marks with you. Copy an image or export PNG, JPEG, WebP or PDF. Crop local exports to the detail you need.

Redact private pixels before sharing. Blur is visual softening, not secure redaction.
